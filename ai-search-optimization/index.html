Your next customer may ask ChatGPT or Google before they ever visit a website. We help your business give those systems—and the buyer behind the question—a clearer answer.

Swiss Sales is an AI search and organic sales growth partnership for established businesses. We use generative engine optimization (GEO) to help buyers discover products and businesses through large language models (LLMs), including ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. The objective is attributable sales, not simply more mentions.

What changes for your business

We start with the exact questions your ideal buyers ask. Then we test whether an AI assistant can find your business, understand what sets it apart, and support a recommendation with credible evidence.

  • Product and offer pages with the specifications and proof buyers need
  • Technical SEO, accessible pages, crawlability and structured data where it fits
  • Independent references, accurate company information, and documented partner results
  • Separate tracking for AI mentions, citations, qualified visits, and attributed sales

AI search revenue in practice

PackagingSupplies.com reports approximately $12,000 in monthly first-time sales from LLM referrals and $5,000 from Google AI Overview. Including repeat purchases from AI-acquired customers, the reported share reaches about 15% of monthly sales. These are reported attribution figures for one partnership, not a forecast.

What we do not promise

Nobody can buy a guaranteed recommendation from ChatGPT, Gemini or Google. Models change, answers vary, and a mention is not a sale. We focus on the things your business can improve and measure.

How we know if it is working

We track important buyer questions, whether your brand appears, whether a source is cited, and what happens when people reach your site. Then we connect that evidence to inquiries, purchases and repeat buying where the data allows.

Is there a strong fit?

We partner with established businesses offering products or expertise their ideal customers actively seek. Our strategy connects AI and organic discovery to qualified sales and repeat purchases. Partnerships begin with a minimum $10,000 retainer, plus commission on sales. We agree on the commercial priorities, define what we can measure, and distinguish verified results from estimates.
