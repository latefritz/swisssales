The business

PackagingSupplies.com serves people and businesses looking for shipping and packaging products. Qualified buying traffic depends on precise specifications, trustworthy product information, and a direct path to purchase.

The strategy

Make PackagingSupplies.com easier to discover when buyers ask Google or AI which packaging products to choose. Give those buyers precise product information and buying guidance so they can order with confidence. Then build on that first purchase through email and repeat-order journeys. The aim is simple: earn the recommendation, win the sale, and give the customer a reason to return.

What the numbers do—and don't—say

The two reported first-time sales figures total approximately $17,000 per month, or roughly 7% of monthly sales. The estimated 15% share includes repeat purchases from AI-acquired customers; it is not an additional 15% on top of the first-time sales. Referral attribution can miss buyers who discover a business through AI and later visit directly. These figures do not establish incremental revenue or prove that a particular optimization caused the sales. See our guide to measuring AI-referred sales.

The lesson

The opportunity is not simply to appear in an AI answer. It's to help the buyer find exactly what they need, place an order, and return when the next need arises.

Reporting and attribution
