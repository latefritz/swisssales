A real client example · Sales by Attraction

From discovery to repeat orders.

A shipping-supplies buyer rarely needs a speech. They need the right product, the right details and confidence they can order it. That makes this ecommerce work a useful example of Sales by Attraction.

The business

PackagingSupplies.com serves people and businesses looking for shipping and packaging products. A useful search experience depends on clear specifications, trustworthy product information and an easy way to buy.

The work

The work has included improving buying guidance and product discoverability, resolving inconsistencies in on-site information, and strengthening email journeys that help customers come back. AI discovery is evaluated alongside conventional search and retention—not treated as a magic sales channel.

What the numbers do—and don't—say

Client-reported attribution previously shared on the Swiss Sales homepage indicated approximately $12,000 per month from LLM referrals and $5,000 from Google AI Overview at the time of reporting. These are snapshots, not independently audited figures or proof that one particular change caused the revenue. Repeat-purchase attribution needs separate care.

The lesson

The opportunity is not simply to appear in an AI answer. It's to help a qualified buyer find a good fit, complete an order and have a reason to return.

What would strengthen this case study

A dated reporting period, source-of-truth analytics screenshots, an agreed attribution definition, before-and-after baselines and client sign-off would allow a fuller, more rigorous account. Until then, the example is deliberately limited.
