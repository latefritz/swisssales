Swiss Sales / Our Method
The Swiss Sales method · Sales by Attraction

Find the right customer. Earn the next sale.

We identify where your ideal buyers are looking, show them what sets your offer apart, and turn their first purchase into lasting customer value.

1. Understand what the business sells

We start with the buyers who produce the best business, their questions, their objections, and how a purchase really happens. Without that, the rest is guesswork.

2. Make the answer easy to find

We improve the pages, facts, technical signals and outside evidence that help people discover and understand your business through organic and AI-assisted search.

3. Make the next step obvious

Someone who arrives ready to buy should not have to fight the website. We remove friction, clarify the offer and make the next step easy.

4. Earn repeat business

Email, SMS, and carefully timed customer journeys build on the trust earned with the first purchase. We test and improve based on actual sales—not vanity numbers.

5. Measure, learn, improve

We separate what we can prove from what we suspect. AI citations, visits, leads, first purchases and repeat revenue are related, but they are not the same metric.

Is there a strong fit?

We partner with established businesses offering products or expertise their ideal customers actively seek. Our work connects AI and organic discovery to qualified sales and repeat purchases. Partnerships begin with a minimum $10,000 retainer, plus commission on sales. We agree on the commercial priorities, define what we can measure, and distinguish verified results from estimates.
