About Swiss Sales · Sales by Attraction

Good work should speak for itself.

Swiss Sales is built on a simple idea: if you have something worth buying, you should be easier to find by the people who already want it.

Why the name

The idea grew from an appreciation for the kind of care and attention people associate with Swiss craftsmanship. Do the work well. Be clear about what you offer. Let the quality become the recommendation.

What makes the work different

We connect being found with being chosen—and then with being chosen again. That means AI discovery, organic SEO, useful buying content and the customer relationships that keep a business healthy.

Who it is for

We work best with established businesses that have real demand, a useful product or service, and the ability to serve more of the right customers. We're not selling a shortcut or a guaranteed AI ranking.

A considered partnership

The work is hands-on and selective. Engagements begin with a minimum $10,000 retainer plus a sales-based commission arrangement, with exact terms agreed for each partnership.

Is this a good fit?

We work with established businesses that have something people already want—and are ready to make it easier to find, buy and buy again. Our partnerships start at a minimum $10,000 retainer, plus commission on sales. We will be clear about what is measurable, what is uncertain and where the real opportunity is.
