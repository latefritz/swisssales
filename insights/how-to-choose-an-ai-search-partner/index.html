How to choose an AI search partner.

Five questions to ask before investing in generative engine optimization (GEO).

If you're paying someone to help grow sales through AI, what should you ask before you begin? Start with these five questions.

1. What results can you show?

Ask which business they helped, how much it sold and where the numbers came from. A screenshot of an AI answer is useful, but it's not a sales report.

2. Which buyer questions matter?

Ask which questions your best buyers ask before they spend money. A buyer comparing two brands or looking for a product within a budget is different from someone just doing research.

3. What will actually change?

Ask which pages need to change and why. Are buyers missing facts that would help them choose? Can AI read your product pages? More articles alone won't solve those problems.

4. How will you measure sales?

Track how often AI names your brand, how often buyers visit, what they order and whether they come back. Agree on which sales count and avoid counting the same order twice. See our measurement guide.

5. How do both partners benefit?

Ask what you'll pay up front and how any commission is worked out. Agree on which orders count and what happens when a buyer returns an item. Swiss Sales uses a retainer plus commission partnership model; it is not commission-only.
