Measurement guide · Sales by Attraction

Visibility is a start. Sales are the measure.

AI visibility gets attention. Sales tell you whether that attention matters. Once you begin earning AI recommendations, track the sales that follow. Separate recommendations, citations, visits, and sales before deciding what is working.

1. Count where you're recommended

Choose real buyer questions, test them consistently, and record whether your brand appears. Track the model, prompt, date and answer. Answers can change, so test more than once.

2. Count where you're cited

A brand can appear without a link, or a link can appear without a strong recommendation. Keep citations and recommendations as separate columns.

3. Count the visits you can identify

Use analytics referral sources and tagged campaign links where possible. Some AI-driven discovery arrives without a clean referral signal, so describe these figures as identifiable or attributed traffic.

4. Count orders and new customers

Connect identifiable sessions to leads or orders, and document your attribution window. Avoid treating platform-reported revenue as proof of incremental growth.

5. Watch repeat purchases

The first order is just the beginning. Look at how customers acquired through identifiable AI referrals reorder over time, with the same cautions used for other marketing channels.

A simple dashboard

Start with five measures: recommendation share, citation share, identifiable AI visits, first orders and repeat-customer revenue. Write down what each number means.
