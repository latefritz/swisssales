Measurement guide · Sales by Attraction

Visibility is a start. Sales are the measure.

How much did the people who found you through AI spend in your store? Once you begin earning AI recommendations, track the sales that follow. Track recommendations, links, visits and orders on their own.

1. Count where you're recommended

Ask AI the same buying questions over time. Write down the tool, date, question and answer. Note how often your brand comes up.

2. Count where you're cited

AI might name your brand without linking to your site. Or it might link to your site without recommending a product. Keep track of both.

3. Count the visits you can identify

Check where visits come from in Google Analytics. You can also use tagged links where they are available. Some buyers find you through AI and visit your site later, so not every visit can be traced to AI.

4. Count orders and new customers

Use your store's sales data and analytics to see which visits led to orders. Note how long after a visit you count a sale. Sales traced to AI are not necessarily extra sales the store would not have made otherwise.

5. Watch repeat purchases

Track what each group of new AI buyers spends after their first order.

A simple dashboard

Track five things: how often AI names you, how often it links to you, visits you can trace to AI, first orders and repeat sales. Write down what each number means.
