Can AI find a reason to recommend you?

You cannot force ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Perplexity or any other AI assistant to recommend your business. But you can make it easier for a buyer—and a search system—to understand exactly who you help and why.

Start with the question, not the keyword

Write down 20 questions an ideal customer would ask before buying. Include category searches, comparisons, price questions and objections. Check what answers appear, and record the date and tool. One answer is not a benchmark.

Make the facts easy to verify

Put essential product details, service scope, real expertise, limitations and contact details on accessible pages. Avoid hiding your best information in images or vague slogans.

Give independent sources a reason to mention you

Publish verified client examples, documented methods, and original research that independent sources have a reason to reference. Paid endorsements and manufactured mentions are not a substitute for reputation.

Measure recommendation and revenue separately

Track when the brand is named, when the site is cited, and when visits or sales can be attributed. Not every AI conversation creates a website click, and no single analytics report captures everything.

Keep conventional SEO in the picture

Crawlable HTML, descriptive titles, strong internal linking, accurate information, and legitimate references still matter. An llms.txt file or extra schema markup is not a guaranteed ranking switch.
