Can AI find a reason to recommend you?

You cannot force ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Perplexity or any other AI assistant to recommend your business. But you can make it easier for a buyer—and a search system—to understand exactly who you help and why.

How can your business get recommended by ChatGPT? Start with the questions buyers ask, give clear answers on your website, and build a name that trusted sources can back up. No one can guarantee an AI recommendation.

1. Start with what buyers want.

Think of a Swiss watchmaker. A buyer may ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity:

  • Which Swiss watches are best for daily wear?
  • Which mechanical watches cost less than $5,000?
  • Which watch brands offer long-term repairs and service?

Each question points to a different buying need. Write down 20 questions your ideal buyers ask. Test them and note which brands and sources appear.

2. Give buyers the facts.

A watchmaker can list the movement, case size, materials, price, water resistance and warranty on each product page. Explain who each watch is best for and how it differs from other models.

Put these facts in text, not just in photos. Keep prices and stock details up to date. Make it easy for buyers to compare and buy.

3. Make your pages easy to find.

Give each product its own clear title and page. Link to it from the right collection. Check that search engines can read the page. Use accurate product structured data (code that helps search tools read product facts).

Good organic SEO supports AI search too. No special file or code can guarantee a recommendation.

4. Give others a reason to praise you.

Trusted reviews, trade publications and real customer results can help buyers learn why your business stands out. Share facts and original research that others can check and use.

Do not invent reviews or pay for praise that looks independent. Build a name worth recommending.

5. Measure sales.

Test the same buyer questions over time. Record when AI names your brand or links to your site. Track visits, first orders and repeat purchases separately. Answers change, and not every AI buyer clicks a link.

Read our guide to tracking AI sales for more detail.

Can AI recommendations lead to sales?

Yes. PackagingSupplies.com reported about $12,000 a month in first-time sales from LLM referrals and $5,000 from Google AI Overview. With repeat orders included, customers gained through AI made up about 15% of monthly sales.

Reported November 2026 figures for one business. These estimates are not independently audited and do not guarantee future results.
