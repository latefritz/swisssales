The thinking behind the name

What I noticed in Switzerland.

A different kind of selling

When I visited Switzerland, I noticed something almost everywhere. Things were clean, well made, and carefully done. Nothing seemed to be begging for attention. It just felt like quality.

Think of a Swiss watch or a great Swiss-made tool. You don't need somebody pushing you to want it. The reputation does a lot of the work. You already have a feeling about what you're getting.

That's what I want for the businesses I work with. Not manipulating people into buying. Making it easier for the right people to recognize a good solution—and want it for themselves.

That's the Swiss Sales idea. Sales by Attraction.

An example from real client work

The first sale is good.
The next one is even better.

This is what makes the approach interesting. AI can introduce someone who already wants what you sell. And when that first experience is good, the customer can come back on their own.

A real client example · PackagingSupplies.com

From virtually no AI-attributed sales to a growing source of new and repeat buyers.

Today, the business sees around $12,000 a month from LLM referrals and $5,000 from Google AI Overview—about $17,000 in first-time purchases, or roughly 7% of monthly sales.

But it doesn't stop there. When those customers come back and buy again, AI-acquired customers now account for about 15% of monthly sales—and that share is growing.

Client-reported attributed results. These numbers are not a guarantee of what another business will see.

How I work

Start with the sales.
Work backward.

I don't want to hand you a stack of buzzwords or a list of things to do just because they're trendy. I want to find the few things that will make a difference to your business.

01

Understand what's already working.

What's selling? Who buys it? Where do your best customers come from now? What happens after somebody places an order?

02

Fix what's getting in the way.

Sometimes that means better product pages or making the business more visible in search and AI answers. Sometimes it's a clearer buying experience or a smarter follow-up after the sale.

03

Build on the results.

We'll pay attention to sales, not just clicks. Keep the work that produces customers. Improve what doesn't. And look for the next sensible opportunity to grow.
