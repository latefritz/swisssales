Can AI search generate sales?

Yes. When someone asks an AI assistant to recommend a product, supplier or solution, the answer can introduce a business at the point of buying intent. A recommendation may lead to a website visit, inquiry or purchase. The opportunity is to earn that discovery with accurate information and credible evidence, then make the purchase straightforward.

Choose a partner by commercial outcomes.

Ask whether a prospective partner can distinguish AI mentions from referral visits, first-time sales and repeat orders. Look for documented results, a clear attribution method and a strategy suited to your buyers. A ranking report alone cannot establish revenue growth.

Swiss Sales combines AI search optimization with organic search and customer retention. For online stores, our ecommerce AI search strategy focuses on product discovery and buying decisions.

Commercial evidence, not just visibility.
